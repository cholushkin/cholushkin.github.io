# About

Hi, I’m Maxim.

I’m a gameplay developer and generalist working across code, systems, and content — from early prototypes to complete products.

I explore ideas by building them, usually through small, focused experiments in graphics, interaction, demos, and tooling.

This site is a working space:

- **Blog** — updates, thoughts, and progress on projects and ideas  
- **Snapshots** — quick captures of things that worked or looked interesting  
- **Sandbox** — experiments, prototypes, and R&D  
- **Cheatsheets** — distilled references I actually use  

My interests:
- gameplay systems and rapid prototyping  
- game design and concepts  
- procedural and AI-driven content (art, music, meshes, levels, textures)  
- rendering and visual debugging  
- worldbuilding and systemic environments  

I focus on full-cycle development — taking ideas from rough experiments to finished games.

I usually have many game ideas in motion, with 1–3 larger projects evolving in parallel.  
Long-term, I aim to build a studio where I can bring my core game ideas to life.
